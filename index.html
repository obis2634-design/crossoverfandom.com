How we keep this a good place to be

Crossover is an adult space. Mature and sexual topics can come up in conversation, and that's allowed within these limits — nothing here is optional.

1

Verified 18+ only

Age is checked at signup. No exceptions, no workarounds — accounts found to belong to a minor are removed immediately.

2

No bullying, ever

No harassment, pile-ons, targeted mockery, or personal attacks — about a person, not just their opinion on a fandom. Leaders and moderators enforce this actively, not just on report.

3

Mature topics, discussed with consent and context

Adult and sexual themes tied to fandom content can be discussed in spaces marked for it. No sexual content involving minors or real people, ever, in any form — that's an instant, permanent ban.

4

Debate the take, not the person

Debate night gets heated — that's the fun of it. Attacking someone personally instead of their argument isn't.

5

Credit the artist

Reposting art or edits without crediting the original creator isn't allowed, on art day or any other day.
